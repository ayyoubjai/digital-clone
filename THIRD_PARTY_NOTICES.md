# Third-party components

This repository integrates, but does not claim ownership of:

- [Ditto Talking Head](https://github.com/antgroup/ditto-talkinghead), included
  as a pinned Git submodule and licensed under Apache License 2.0. Its license
  and notices remain in `vendors/Ditto`.
- [Qwen3-TTS](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base), downloaded
  at runtime. Review its model card and license before redistribution or
  commercial use.
- NVIDIA CUDA, cuDNN, and TensorRT runtime components, distributed under their
  respective NVIDIA terms.
- [MPV](https://mpv.io/), used as the persistent offline media player and
  distributed under its respective open-source licenses by Ubuntu.

Model weights are not committed to this repository.
